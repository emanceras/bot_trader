# Proposed staged statistical admission

Document ID: IV-EVOL-STAT-001 · Version: 1 · Date: 2026-10-27 · English canonical. [Spanish](../../../es/phase-iv/evolution/staged-admission.md).

**Proposed clarification, not enacted amendment or permission to run.** The [accepted E1/E2 design](../protocol.md), [candidate comparisons](../candidates.md), [method limits](../method-evidence.md) and [current acceptance](../acceptance.md) remain authoritative. This document resolves the sequencing question raised in D-039/D-041 for review; it does not edit the protocol or populate the [freeze template](../../../phase-iv/freeze-template.json). E3/E4, Phase V and all financial computation remain pending. No new academic claim or empirical result is introduced.

## Why one unconditional pre-run gate cannot supply all evidence

The protocol requires economically material paired net-return comparisons and development-based dependence/precision assessment before validation. Structural file checks and synthetic execution tests cannot supply the variance of `d_t = r_candidate,t − r_reference,t`; raw coin volatility and a published Sharpe are not substitutes. Requiring a fully estimated paired-return distribution before the first authorized development computation creates a circular dependency.

Recommend **distinct admission manifests for distinct purposes**, with a protected chronological evaluation envelope. This is not permission to leave arbitrary fields blank in an executable financial run. Every binding on which the current computation depends must be fixed first. Values learned legitimately in development may be frozen later for validation; economic hurdles and research limits must not be adjusted to make observed candidates pass.

## Four roles, plus technical admission

These are evidence/access roles, not four universal equal-sized time slices or four new project phases. The uncertainty role uses development material only. A separate untouched calibration segment *inside development* is required if the procedure was revised using the purported outer-fold evidence; it is not the final holdout.

| Gate / role | Inputs permitted | Required bindings before access | Output and stop condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| T — technical admission, within an applicable technical scope | Synthetic fixtures; authorized structural metadata/QA only | Exact source rights/scope and engine versions, timestamp/accounting/identity tests, finite technical work plan | Aptness/limitations, no return evidence. Unsupported data semantics or unresolved accounting prevents dependent financial admission |
| D — bounded exploratory financial development, only after separate authorization | Named development data; past-only inner/outer folds | Approved amendment and precise financial scope; E3 finite trial/compute/acquisition ceiling; E4 primary capital, cash/incremental materiality, C2 tradeoff and risk scenarios; source/universe/USD/cost/engine admission; exact development dates, clocks, recipes, folds, seeds, attempt log and custody of all later data | Registered selection procedures and all negative/failed outcomes. Exhaustion, invalid execution or unresolved data integrity stops; no “winner” claim |
| U — dependence, uncertainty and adequacy assessment, still development | Paired calendar-time net returns of frozen procedures from eligible outer development evaluation; or an untouched development calibration segment when needed | Nuisance-estimation method, proposed block rule/sensitivities, precision and relevant alternative requirements, finite calibration/extension policy, evidence of implementation/null checks | Determine whether enough information remains for independent validation/final. Inadequate/unstable support yields inconclusive/defer, not new significance targets |
| V — independent validation admission | One previously untouched later block | Frozen whole selection/refit procedures, exact comparators and timing; fully bound economic/inference/technical/resource manifests and objective partition rule; verified access log | One admission screen. Reject/inconclusive/defer if a required gate fails; no tuning on this block. Surviving claims only proceed by the prescribed rule |
| F — final holdout | One protected later batch and the matching references | All original final bindings, actual seal/custody, immutable batch/version digests and explicit one-time opening authority | Confirmatory evidence only if all original gates pass. Failure is retained; any scientifically changed version needs independent data |

“V” in this table means validation role; **both D and U are financial work that would require a precisely scoped Phase V authorization**. Their naming does not keep them inside a documentation-only Phase IV. Prospective data collection, paper and live each remain separately gated. Existing production permissions are untouched.

## Bindings that cannot wait until results

Before D, choose the estimand, cash/comparator construction, economic margins, capital and risk/cost conventions using the user's informed decisions and source/venue evidence, not observed candidate effects. No universal materiality, capital, drawdown or research-budget value is supplied here. Freeze mechanism-justified horizon/lookbacks, universe eligibility and finite variants before dependent fits. Distinguish explanatory exploratory notes from new executable choices; both consume the declared investigation budget when used for selection.

Record planned and maximum fits/refits/ablations/seeds/stresses, attempted and failed runs, and implementation repairs. The existing **26 base-slot ceiling (8 rule/reference + 18 C3 slots)** is unchanged and not a full fit count. This proposal adds no models, markets, opening-candle strategy, interval grid, news inputs or unallocated reserve slots. C1/C3 remain primary, C2 secondary, C4/C5 reserves. Extra scope needs a prospective reason and approved finite envelope; cheap computation does not enlarge it.

Before any financial development access, designate and protect the later evaluation envelope using *coverage metadata only*. No validation/final price charts, returns, feature distributions or AI summaries may inform its choice. The exact validation/final split may be determined later by a **predeclared deterministic adequacy rule using D/U and sealed coverage metadata**, not by favorable periods. Bind the maximum acquisition/evaluation calendar envelope and the rule for an impossible split first; if insufficient, defer rather than quietly selecting a different cycle. Choosing fixed full dates from metadata in advance is also valid, but can leave the comparison underpowered. Both options need explicit amendment review because current acceptance requires complete bindings before all financial runs.

The inspected Kraken Q3 sample remains exposed QA material. Previously viewed historical results, published narratives or discretionary chart inspection must be declared; being chronologically later does not automatically make a block independent or unknown. If a suitable unseen envelope cannot be established, document that limitation and postpone confirmation. This proposal does not authorize future collection to solve it.

## How U avoids becoming another search stage

1. Preserve the whole causal selection procedure. Outer development folds can estimate its out-of-sample behavior only if their outcomes did not tune that procedure. If inspected outer results inspired a revision, label that track exploratory and consume budget; do not present its apparent uncertainty as independently calibrated evidence for the revision.
2. Prefer a frozen procedure assessed on untouched development calibration data when that contamination exists. It must lie inside the authorized development envelope; dates/coverage determined without outcomes, labels fully available, purges/embargo and shared asset cutoffs preserved. This segment is then exposed development data and cannot later serve as validation/final.
3. Estimate paired dependence/variance and support by contiguous segments, not independent assets, trades or concatenated nonadjacent folds. Allow prespecified causal refits, preserving cross-strategy/asset dependence. Block selection, finite short/base/long sensitivity and failures are logged; insufficient tail/regime support or unstable assumptions remains a limitation.
4. Desired confidence precision and the economically relevant alternative are specified before U outcomes, linked to fixed materiality and decision consequences. Estimate uncertainty in nuisance parameters and stress plausible dependence/nonstationarity; an estimated required sample size is not a power guarantee. Do not define the alternative as the best observed development effect or lower the margin to fit available data. An unused development calibration block reduces selection feedback but cannot certify future stationarity.
5. U does not select another candidate, seed, block size or regime because its p-value is favorable. If the diagnostics require a scientific revision, return to D under an explicit remaining/amended budget, record exposure and require new independent calibration where needed. Never repeat this cycle without a finite approved stopping rule.

Keep the existing paired stationary-block inference, applicability checks and synthetic dependent-null calibration requirement. Calibration tests for the statistical implementation are future technical tests; none is executed here. For a mean-differential adequacy discussion, long-run variance includes serial covariance, and precision scales with effective information rather than raw bar count. No normal approximation, IID formula or fixed minimum sample is silently substituted for the approved method.

## Validation, final inference and outcomes preserved

The approved primary family stays **C1, C3-I, C3-X**, each requiring both incremental and absolute-cash gates. Keep `p_claim = max(p_gain, p_cash)`, Holm over three claims with unrun/ineligible claims retained as p=1, the accepted 0.05 family level and 9,999 bootstrap draws. Those values are inherited approvals, not newly selected thresholds. Their validity still depends on adequate marginal tests, temporal assumptions and independent evaluation; staged permission cannot repair invalid inference.

The one-time validation screen is not a second opportunity to claim final success. Final inference remains one authorized batch on frozen procedures, with prespecified causal refits only. No model choice, changed costs, capital, cash vehicle, horizon or block rule may depend on validation/final outcomes. Same-data fixes that have observed results require a contamination review; failed execution does not reset the exposure counter. Technical repeatability is not independent evidence.

Keep original outcome categories: technically invalid; not evaluable/deferred; inconclusive; evidence against the specified material claim; or support for that scoped claim. A non-rejection is not proof of no alpha, and a technical pass or user authorization is not proof of economic value. Stopping without an admitted candidate is an acceptable project outcome.

## Proposed records and approval delta

Use small immutable linked manifests rather than additional empty runtime services: `technical_admission`, `development_admission`, `uncertainty_review`, `validation_freeze`, `final_opening`. These are proposed record roles, not new implemented schemas. Each cites parent digests, purpose/access, exposed partitions, unresolved fields with dependencies, authorized actor, source/engine/code versions, attempt budget and outcomes. A later stage cannot widen an earlier permission by changing a flag.

If approved later, the explicit amendment would change only the **timing of completeness** in protocol §§3/4/7/8, acceptance's “before a Phase V financial run” gate and freeze-manifest structure. It must leave estimands, comparisons, selection restrictions, E1/E2, economic criteria, multiplicity, failed-run retention and final custody intact. Present a concrete redline and stage-specific populated manifests before execution. This delivery preserves those files byte for byte and requests no blanket Phase V approval.

**Recommendation:** approve this sequencing principle only after reviewing its binding/access distinctions; decide any actual D/U authorization separately with source/engine admission and E3/E4 populated. Alternative: retain the current unconditional financial gate and remain at conditional methodological readiness. Neither option requires another general scientific literature search or arbitrary numeric defaults now.
