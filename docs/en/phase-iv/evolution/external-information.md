# External information — versioned events before predictive models

Document ID: IV-EVOL-EXT-001 · Version: 1 · Date: 2026-10-27 · English canonical. [Spanish](../../../es/phase-iv/evolution/external-information.md).

## Recommendation and evidence boundary

Prepare a common event/provenance contract with typed extensions for publications, numerical releases and chain observations. Do not introduce a mandatory LLM, separate news platform or universal sentiment score. Existing [news research](../../../research/news-events.md), [source assessment](../../../research/news-data-sources.md), [coverage audit](../../../research/coverage-audit.md) and [IV news gate](../data-execution.md) already require incremental value and point-in-time availability. This annex makes their future architectural implications explicit; it adds no scientific hypothesis, corpus download, model execution or slot to the 26-specification wave. C1/C3, C2 and C4/C5 priorities and pending E3/E4 remain unchanged.

Two official documentation pages were retrieved on 2026-10-26: SEC developer APIs and Ethereum JSON-RPC. Directed review of their cached sections and selected prior macro/news documentation on 2026-10-27 is now recorded; no earlier read timestamp is reconstructed from retrieval alone. [Receipts](../../../phase-iv/evolution/external-sources.jsonl) preserve dates, hashes, examined scope and access limits. Documentary API behavior is not measured latency, a licensed corpus or evidence of predictive advantage. No filing, news article corpus, economic series or blockchain response was downloaded.

No source above has demonstrated incremental profitability. Corporate, macro, regulatory and technology events can affect several instruments, but the direction, mechanism and delay are hypotheses. A technology announcement does not imply that every token or related listed company has the same exposure. Entity/security mapping and shared-event clustering must precede cross-market evaluation.

## Minimal future contract and processing boundary

Reuse the [temporal proposal](temporal.md). Do not force every event into an OHLC bar or require every source to supply fields it never observed.

| Contract component | Proposed semantics and invariant |
|---|---|
| Identity and relations | Provider-independent internal event/version IDs plus source IDs, filing accession or chain block/transaction/log identity. Link original, revision, translation, retraction and related events without overwriting them. A source ID's scope is explicit |
| Clocks and known versions | Event/effective time, original publication time with precision/timezone, provider first-seen time, local receipt, ingestion and availability evidence. Unknown values remain null; publication, SEC acceptance, retrieval and model inference clocks are not interchangeable. Preserve knowledge time as well as effective time |
| Scheduled versus realized data | Versioned schedule/consensus snapshots known before the event; first released value, unit, reference period, subsequent revisions and expectation source. Distinguish surveyed consensus, market-implied expectations and a model forecast. Do not join a later consensus to the release |
| Entity mapping | Versioned issuer, instrument, venue, chain/network and protocol identifiers; mapping's effective and known times, confidence, source and unresolved ambiguity. A current ticker, contract address label or corporate parent relationship cannot be retroactively applied by default |
| Content, rights and lineage | Content/metadata hash, language, original source/version, retrieval provenance and permitted storage/model/derived-output uses. Rights-limited deletion uses an auditable tombstone and retained permitted provenance; a hash neither grants rights nor always permits retaining derived personal information |
| Derived features | Parent event versions, causal transform/model snapshot, tokenizer/prompt/configuration, inference/computation timing, units and feature availability. Sentiment, novelty and relevance are estimates, distinct from observed release values and human decisions |
| Chain state | Chain ID, block hash/height, parent hash, transaction/log identity, observed state/finality status, local first observation and removal/correction events. Finality qualification is a versioned policy, not an unconditional guarantee |
| Quality and missingness | Coverage, duplicates, late messages, conflicts, withdrawals and uncertain times remain explicit. Retraction is new information; it cannot silently rewrite what the old strategy knew |

Recommended flow: licensed immutable source versions where permitted → normalized event envelopes → causal entity/group mapping → optional typed features → availability-aware join with market observations → frozen research procedure. Audit records persist links to evidence. Structured retrieval and simple numerical/lexical features precede semantic search, RAG or LLM inference unless a concrete requirement justifies complexity. News text is untrusted input; it cannot execute tools, alter risk policies or grant permissions.

Separate an **as-observed** view for replay from a **latest-corrected** view for retrospective description. A historical corpus lacking first-seen versions cannot be made equivalent to a direct feed by assigning today's download time as historical reception. At most, admit a supported slower availability bound for a specified use, or classify the question not evaluable. Replaying an authorized future captured stream could test transformations; it would not itself establish historical completeness or identical fills. No stream capture is activated now.

## Future incremental tests and explicit failure conditions

A later separately budgeted hypothesis would distinguish anticipation, reaction after receipt, and risk management. The following are proposed comparisons, not new authorized experiments:

- Common price/volume/risk baseline; add known calendar only; add numerical surprise only when a pre-release consensus exists; compare text-only and hybrid information on matched target times, capital, execution and finite search budgets. Apply the same incremental and cash logic where a new economic claim is approved, without silently expanding the current Holm family.
- Compare simple lexical/statistical text features with a frozen NLP/LLM using identical input versions; ablate source coverage, text, entity mapping, novelty and each on-chain block separately. Retain useful additive information if interactions fail; do not discard a feature solely because its standalone strategy loses.
- Group shared events/republications across assets in evaluation splits; fit mappings/statistical transforms only with permitted training information. A conservative later contamination audit may invalidate a split but cannot provide future grouping knowledge to the earlier signal.
- Freeze model/version/knowledge-cutoff information known or unknown, prompts and inference budget. A chronological split does not erase pretraining contamination. If retrospective contamination cannot be ruled out, qualify the claim and require separately authorized prospective evidence before relying on it.
- Vary only predeclared plausible feed/model/execution delays and cost scenarios; compare decisions from first versions and revisions as distinct analyses. Reject a claimed immediate reaction if its information was not yet available; classify net value as unsupported if the incremental improvement disappears after attainable latency/costs, equivalent baseline information or independent validation.

Unchanged outcomes, silent releases, false rumors, adverse periods and failed feeds belong in the eligible history. Thousands of syndicated headlines are not thousands of independent shocks. A change in predictive score is not economic causality or a realizable portfolio benefit. No adverse finding creates a licence to search unlimited new sources/prompts/windows.

## Costs, deferrals and re-entry conditions

Separate acquisition/rights for historical versions and consensus, recurring feed/indexer/node charges, storage/egress, inference, engineering/maintenance and execution costs. A free official document can still require expensive timing reconstruction; a premium feed can fail the first-version requirement. Reuse the dated source-price evidence for orientation, then obtain a use-specific quotation before any spend. No numeric budget or minimum capital is proposed here.

**Prepare as documentation now:** these contracts, source-level coverage/licence questions and independent acceptance cases. **Defer implementation/acquisition:** feeds, node/indexer infrastructure, entity graph, sentiment pipelines and autonomous retrieval. **Do not add currently:** a universal news sentiment trading rule, automatic LLM fine-tuning, a permanent social-data archive inconsistent with rights, or a new scientific family merely for having an event source. Reconsider only when a specific hypothesis has a finite incremental comparison, eligible timestamps/versions, attainable cost and separately authorized resources.
