# Financial engine comparison and proposed admission tests

Document ID: EV-ENGINE-001 · Version: 1 · Date: 2026-10-27 · English canonical. [Spanish](../../../es/phase-iv/evolution/engines.md).

## Recommendation and evidence boundary

Keep **NautilusTrader and LEAN as the short list**, with no final selection. Nautilus has useful measured evidence from the authorized isolated 1.231.0 probe; LEAN has a documented broader asset/brokerage ecosystem but no local execution evidence. Prior implementation effort is not proof of superiority. Freqtrade is a reserve for an explicitly compatible crypto candle workflow, not the default general platform engine. Do not install another engine, migrate production or build a financial adapter in this documentary task.

A material qualification to the prior provisional Nautilus preference is its maintenance line: the examined upstream `develop` README says v1 receives only critical security backports while v2 is a release candidate with a different API, and warns against production RC use [E1]. A future dependency upgrade alone would not resolve release support, adapter semantics or recovery. Before either finalist is admitted, choose an exact maintained release/commit and matching documentation. The stable 1.231.0 results remain valid for that tested version; they are not silently transferred to v2.

Evidence labels below: **observed** means the bounded project synthetic probe, **documented** means official inspected statements, and **unverified** means no project demonstration. Marketing performance and supported-venue lists do not replace tests. This review reuses [III engine comparison](../../architecture/engines.md), [architectural supplement](../../architecture/supplement.md), [A/B results](../admission.md) and prior official licence/fill-model sources. Three additional documentation snapshots were preserved by the interrupted review and read during this documentary review; no new download, installation, benchmark or financial experiment was performed by this resumed comparison. [Source receipts](../../../phase-iv/evolution/engine-sources.jsonl).

## Requirements: gates rather than scores

Mandatory means necessary before the particular dependent use, not a requirement to implement every market or live control in the first wave. A failed mandatory property cannot be offset by speed or more connectors. Missing evidence is **unverified**, not a failure probability. Crypto C1/C3, C2 and C4/C5, the conditional 26-slot ceiling and E3/E4 remain unchanged.

| Requirement | Gate and reason | NautilusTrader | QuantConnect LEAN | Freqtrade |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Causal availability, data/decision clocks, deterministic frozen transforms | Mandatory for financial research; no future-input or bar-close fill leakage | Event-driven; bounded queued-latency fixture observed, not complete causal adapter admission | Event-driven and timestamp conventions documented; actual adapter unverified | Candle timing and callbacks documented; whole-dataframe/preprocessing use must be constrained |
| Instrument identity, quote/base/settlement currencies and unsupported-capability rejection | Mandatory for admitted products; architecture must not hardcode crypto | Multiasset/venue design documented; only invented spot/USD pair observed | Multiple security/brokerage models documented; actual product admission pending | Crypto-focused spot/futures framework; other markets would require substantial accommodation |
| Orders, fills, cancellations, fees, minimums and cash/inventory accounting | Mandatory for initial spot; exact rounding rules and independent oracle | Native IOC partial/cancel, rejections, maker/taker fees and cash/position reconciliation observed | Configurable order/fee/fill models documented; no local oracle comparison | Backtest assumptions include requested-price fills within candle range and no slippage [E2]; fee/precision features do not establish realistic execution |
| Liquidity/capacity, stale information and uncertain outcomes | Mandatory for whichever execution model is admitted; support explicit no-fill/unknown states | L1 market fallback failed the conservative finite-visible-size assertion; L2/queue model not tested | Default market impact absent and immediate/stale fills possible [E3]; custom models needed for dependent claims | Finer detail candles reduce ambiguity but do not reconstruct order-book depth, queue priority or partial execution |
| Common signal/decision logic across historical and future direct modes | Mandatory architecture property; equivalent inputs/decisions, not identical fills | Shared strategy code documented; transport/persistence/venue behavior explicitly differs | Shared algorithm ecosystem; live/history data and model differences explicitly documented | Backtest/dry-run/live framework, but intra-candle and current precision assumptions differ |
| Shared capital, multiple strategies and attribution | Mandatory before multiple strategies share an account; not proved by running several bots | Multiple strategies/venues documented; project netting, ownership and allocation adapter unverified | Portfolio/framework composition documented; brokerage aggregate position is not virtual strategy ownership | Multiple strategy evaluation/bot processes is not a common-capital allocator or independent account segregation |
| Persisted order/fill state, restart, external reconciliation and unknown-position protection | Mandatory before prospective operation; deterministic historical restart required for resumable research | Probe preserves a trace after exit with open position; **no checkpoint restoration or pending-order recovery demonstrated** | State/Object Store/history guidance [E3] is not a tested recovery guarantee | SQLite/operational persistence documented in prior review; complete recovery and shared-account reconciliation unverified |
| Versioned inputs/outputs, reproducibility, substitution and denied live dispatch | Mandatory for every admitted adapter | Needs project adapter; existing synthetic `Execution → Fill` cannot represent full lifecycle | Needs project adapter and .NET/Python boundary | Needs project adapter without adopting its optimizer, UI or live defaults |
| Session calendars, corporate actions, derivative lifecycle, cross-market assets | Desirable extensibility now; mandatory before each corresponding future product | Multiasset design documented; release/adapter-specific support to verify | Strong documentary match for broader asset/account/calendar models; not a measured correctness ranking | Not its natural general-market scope; retain only where crypto workflow actually fits |
| Throughput, latency, resources and operability | Desired performance within a mechanism-derived budget; no universal fastest-engine objective | 10,000 invented ticks without orders measured only as smoke test; not strategy throughput | No comparable local benchmark | No comparable local benchmark; speed cannot repair fill assumptions |

“No market impact by default” and “unverified recovery” are not unique Nautilus defects. All candidates need product-appropriate source and execution assumptions. A default convenience model cannot be certified by agreeing with another engine that makes the same simplification.

The native L1 market fallback failure remains an adverse result. A passing native IOC partial-fill/cancel case does not authorize replacing a market order with IOC, inserting a wrapper fill, or changing the scientific execution policy to obtain a pass.

## Proposed common synthetic comparison — not executed or installed now

Use one immutable, entirely invented fixture bundle and an engine-independent Decimal cash/quantity/order-state oracle. Fix required order semantics, event ordering, fee rounding, data freshness/availability and simulated depth *before* outputs are inspected. Maintain two tracks: reproduce the existing B cases as regression evidence; test the broader required semantics below. A wrapper must report native capability, configured model, wrapper-supplied behavior or unsupported case. It must not manufacture a native pass by injecting the expected fill.

| Case | Common input and acceptance criterion | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| T1 — availability and session boundary | Delayed, out-of-order and corrected invented events; close arrives after interval end; versioned DST/holiday fixture. No decision consumes unavailable values, and known timezone/calendar mapping is exact | Identical admitted information must yield identical transform/decision digests. Execution can differ only according to declared model |
| T2 — spot accounting and currencies | Scripted buys/sells, maker/taker rates, partial quantities, precision/minimums and distinct USD/USDT balances; independent arithmetic including free/reserved cash | Fees charged once; conservation and positions reconcile. No implicit USDT=USD or missing conversion fabrication |
| T3 — finite liquidity and cancellation | Fully specified synthetic depth, limited available quantity, IOC/GTC/FOK where supported, cancellation before/after fill and refusal of unsupported semantics | No fill beyond the fixture's admitted liquidity/model; quantities and remaining/canceled amounts conserved. For bare L1, declare a conservative policy or inadequacy, not unseen depth knowledge |
| T4 — data gaps and exit failure | Sequence gap, stale quote, instrument withdrawal and rejected exit | Prevent new dependent decisions/unsafe fills; preserve stranded exposure and explicit unresolved state. No fabricated terminal liquidation or silent cash conversion |
| T5 — duplicates and attribution | Replayed command ID, repeated fill ID, two strategies sharing account/instrument and capital | No double reservation/fee/fill; broker net position reconciles to virtual attribution without multiplying capital. Ownership conflicts handled by versioned policy |
| T6 — interruption and reconciliation | Controlled crash before acknowledgment, after fill but before local checkpoint, and during cancel; persistent fixture broker state and journal | Restart reconciles orders **and** positions **and** balances, deduplicates events and blocks new risk until uncertainty resolved. Snapshot/replay of a different fixture does not pass this test |
| T7 — reproducibility and failure containment | Fresh process, checkpoint continuation, bounded malformed input and unavailable worker, same fixture manifest | Same permitted decisions/accounting, retained failed attempts, version mismatch rejected, no financial network/live dispatch |
| T8 — measured resource envelope | Identical event shapes, order density, instrument/strategy counts and data model; separately measure import/startup, serialization, processing, disk/RAM and latency tails | Compare actual resource cost at a fixed workload; do not confuse bars with book deltas or a no-order smoke test with full load. Set acceptability from mechanism/resources later, not arbitrary throughput now |

Simulation results need not match at every fill when assumptions differ. First compare under the same explicit model; then explain unavoidable/model-specific differences without majority voting or tuning tolerances until agreement. Numerical tolerances, if needed, derive from declared currency/instrument precision and arithmetic, not observed errors. Calendar cases are infrastructure tests, not authorization of a New York opening strategy. No financial data, strategy fitting or live capture is needed for these proposed tests.

A future approval request should identify exact candidates/versions, native installation method and licences, finite fixture list, expected storage/compute and stopping criteria. Initially compare the two finalists only if their maintained releases can meet mandatory requirements without distorting the protocol. Do not install Freqtrade/vectorbt/Backtrader just to increase the number tested. If one finalist fails a decisive gate, retain the adverse result and test the alternative; additional attempts require a concrete unresolved fact and a bounded amendment.

## Provisional choice and alternatives

**No final engine winner is justified yet.** Retain the existing Nautilus environment as evidence, not a sunk-cost commitment. It is the least new installation work for additional authorized synthetic checks, while its v1 support horizon and dependency transition materially weaken a permanent selection. LEAN deserves a focused comparative admission if the project chooses to resolve those uncertainties: its broader asset/account model is relevant to future extensibility, but its default fills, mixed-runtime integration and data/live differences remain costs to measure. Prefer whichever satisfies the same mandatory cases with the smaller justified integration/maintenance burden; do not weight hypothetical asset breadth above current correctness.

Keep Freqtrade as a scoped reserve when a crypto bar strategy truly fits and realistic execution can be represented without changing its hypothesis. Postpone vectorbt as an optional bounded research calculation library (prior Commons Clause/PRO distinctions remain relevant) and Backtrader pending a concrete maintenance/compatibility need. Neither supplies a reason to replace experiment governance or build a general financial engine ourselves. No second UI, new bot platform or full engine adapter is proposed for this phase.
