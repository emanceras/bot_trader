# Historical and live data — comparative admission proposal

Document ID: EV-DATA-001 · Version: 1 · Date: 2026-10-27 · English canonical. [Spanish](../../../es/phase-iv/evolution/sources.md).

## Decision and evidence boundary

**Do not select a permanent provider from the successful Kraken download.** Maintain direct exchange archives as a low-acquisition-cost route, an event-history aggregator as a conditional route for missing execution/receipt evidence, and future own capture as a route to operator-specific arrival history. These routes can coexist behind source-specific adapters and a common information contract; none is admitted for financial use by this document.

Reuse [feasibility](../feasibility.md), [A/B audit](../admission.md) and their source/rights receipts. Six additional official-site documentation responses were retrieved on 2026-10-26 and preserved before interruption. Directed cached review on 2026-10-27 confirms five documentation pages and one Page Not Found response; the appended events record this review without reconstructing an earlier read timestamp. No response or dataset was downloaded again. [Read/retrieval ledger](../../../phase-iv/evolution/data-sources.jsonl). **O** below means directly observed in the limited A audit; **D** means a documented vendor statement; **U** means not established. Documentation is neither a service guarantee nor a historical completeness test.

C1/C3 remain primary, C2 secondary, C4/C5 reserves; the 26 base specifications are unchanged. Other markets are an architectural extension, not new experimental scope. E3/E4 and Phase V remain pending. No fresh market API request, stream subscription, historical body, service contract or expense occurred.

Historical depth is a **coverage surface by source × instrument identity × event type × time × version**, not a single maximum number of years. Different providers may redistribute the same underlying venue messages; buying two feeds does not automatically provide independent verification. TradingView/chart displays were not admitted as research data: convenience cannot replace explicit export history, field definitions, revision policy and rights. No additional generic provider survey is needed until a concrete gap changes the decision.

## Concrete semantic mismatches to preserve

| Mismatch verified in documentation | Required admission rule |
|---|---|
| Kraken trade `side` is taker side; Coinbase match `side` is maker side [N1,N4] | Keep original side/role and explicitly derive any common aggressor-side field. Never copy an identically named field without its definition |
| Kraken `time_in/time_out` are subscription-acknowledgement clocks [N1,N2] | They cannot populate per-trade collector/operator receipt. Unknown arrival remains unknown |
| Binance `trade` versus `aggTrade`, archived microseconds versus default live milliseconds, incomplete versus closed klines [P3,N3] | Version schema, event identity, units, aggregation recipe and finality. Duplicate counting and future-close leakage are admission failures |
| Kraken CRC covers top 10 levels; Binance snapshot covers a bounded depth and requires update-ID bridging; Coinbase L2 and matches have different delivery properties [N2,N3,N4] | Check each channel's reconstruction and invalidate state on gaps; a top-level checksum or transport promise does not validate unseen depth or reconnect completeness |
| Recovered REST/history messages arrive later than the missed original stream | Store recovery receipt and provenance. Do not backdate them into a historical replay of decisions actually available during the outage |
| Provider-corrected history may differ from the originally received stream [P3,P6] | Preserve both “as received/as known” and “latest corrected” views with explicit intended use. Revised prices cannot silently overwrite causal decisions |

The common temporal architecture is proposed separately; these facts constrain it without selecting a venue, bar interval, trading signal or source of alpha.

## Acquisition plan: coverage first, finite requests

1. **Build a documentary coverage inventory before bodies.** For each proposed source/product, record instrument/venue identifiers, status/migration evidence, earliest/latest documented coverage, holes, event schema/version, currency/units, clocks, adjustments, correction policy, rights and price mechanism. Unknown fields remain unknown. Do not infer history from current tradable symbols. No metadata API was called in this review.
2. **Define the scientific information need.** Separate the broad research universe from executable instruments. Keep trades when exact amounts, event ordering or alternative aggregation justify them; require quotes/depth when the mechanism or sensitivity to costs makes them material. A slower strategy does not automatically need every L3 message, and a fast one cannot infer depth from bars. Preserve news/on-chain/derivative namespaces for future separately admitted sources.
3. **Propose the smallest discriminating acquisition bundle.** Choose windows/instruments by coverage and schema boundaries, inactivity/migrations, currency and known incidents, not observed strategy returns. Compare a direct archive route with an event-history route only where their difference affects eligibility, costs or causal replay. State exact objects/requests, purposes, licence version, bytes or bounded estimates, permitted storage/compute and stopping conditions before asking authorization. Existing Q3 data resolve only their recorded questions; they do not justify extrapolating quality to all years.
4. **Expand validated coverage backwards and across the useful universe in explicit tranches.** Use the earliest reliable legally usable history for each mechanism/instrument, with no arbitrary year cap. Retain manifest-addressed immutable source versions, chunk/partition digests and a missing-data ledger. Acquisition is idempotent; new corrections add versions. Each tranche must add scientifically useful cycles/assets/fields relative to its cost, rather than simply increase bytes. Dates are not selected to rescue results.
5. **Keep evaluation protection separate from storage.** A custodian can check structural metadata without releasing reserved values; loaded/inspected periods and prior knowledge are recorded. Do not combine exposed Q3 with later reserved data and call the resulting panel untouched. Financial development, independent validation and final admission follow the separately reviewed statistical gates.
6. **Plan own live capture only after authorization.** It can record actual receipt/order, disconnects and schema revisions for future replay; it cannot recreate missing historical arrival or past delistings. Collector infrastructure, retention rights, capture scope and monitoring cost need a concrete proposal. No collector, paper process or stream connection starts now.

This plan can support raw event partitions plus derived bars across resolutions without retaining every source forever. Retention follows reproducibility/rights needs; derived artifacts identify exact source versions and transforms. New markets need admitted instrument/calendaring/valuation semantics, not replacement of the platform.

## Admission questions and practical checks proposed, not run

- Reconcile instrument identities/status changes and inactive spans across offered inventories and independent notices; test whether the intended universe is evaluable without silently keeping survivors only.
- Verify original bytes, schema eras, decimals, time units, trade-ID scope and corrections. Preserve genuine repeated trades; distinguish market inactivity from collection loss.
- For an authorized simultaneous capture, replay the same raw messages through the batch and stream paths, compare canonical events and sealed bars, then compare resulting decisions under identical state. Diagnose expected correction/receipt differences separately. Compare execution outcomes statistically under the admitted model; do not demand identical simulated and real fills.
- For book-dependent work, use known-gap/duplicate/out-of-order/disconnect cases and recovery snapshots. Reject apparently valid books with incomplete sequence coverage. Bound queue/hidden-liquidity assumptions separately from displayed depth.
- Confirm attainable per-product historical/current fees, minimums, conversion and receipt latency evidence before cost-sensitive research; a provider's data price and a venue's execution fees are different budgets.

No source currently passes all these gates. **E3** must separately price historical acquisition/licensing, engineering/validation, and recurring feed/storage/operation, in USD with stated conversion/tax and allowance assumptions. **E4** cannot be derived from source popularity or Q3 quality. The next material authorization is a specific finite acquisition or collection/test package once its exact coverage and rights are reviewable; no subscription, full-history download, engine integration or Phase V follows from this proposal.
