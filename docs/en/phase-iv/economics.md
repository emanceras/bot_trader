## 3. Incremental acquisition and shared-disk budget

Coverage is a surface **source × venue × instrument identity × event type × time × version**. Seek earliest reliable history and complementary cycles, current and disappeared assets, migrations and interruptions. A library can cover more sources/periods than the first experiments without changing the confirmatory design. Do not limit its universe to BTC/ETH, current survivors or C1/C3's immediate needs.

Tandas, each independently resumable and auditable:

1. **Catalog and bind:** reuse manifests/objects; enumerate archive identifiers, coverage, lengths, checksums, schema and rights. Record unknown/absent/delisted intervals and source changes. Select whole independently verifiable units using coverage/quality/complementarity, never returns. Freeze exact ordered objects and totals before payload transfer.
2. **Broad granular base:** Kraken's full offered trade history is a tractable initial candidate at the documented size, including nonUSD quotes for future research. Avoid a redundant quarterly download when the same object already exists; overlap across different source vintages is explicit, not double-counted. Keep prior QA originals as evidence.
3. **Binance complement early:** fill missing venue/asset/cycle coverage from earliest admitted objects. If all trades exceed resources, use a clearly labeled broad bar layer with actual quote volume where its schema supports it, then add granular trades by the same coverage rule. Pick the finest viable vetted layer from object sizes/semantics, not the most profitable bar interval. This is a storage representation, not an approved strategy horizon. Record information that bars cannot preserve.
4. **Extend and refresh:** additional venues, inactive/migrated instruments and justified fields; immutable new partitions/revisions, checkpoints and correction links. Do not buy/download a second representation if it adds no information. Retain vendor bars only for distinct definitions or QA; “reproducible from trades” does not imply byte-identical vendor rounding without verification.
