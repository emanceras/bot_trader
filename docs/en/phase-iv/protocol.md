## 2. Unit of inference, objectives and common accounting

The primary observation is a **synchronized calendar-time portfolio return in USD**, after instrument costs, funding/borrow when relevant, conversions, and recurring operating costs allocated over the entire evaluation calendar. It is not an individual fill, overlapping target, asset or profitable trade. Shared timestamps preserve market dependence. Select the reporting interval before returns are inspected, based on data availability and holding/dependence clocks; it need not equal the decision or monitoring interval. Missing observations are neither zeros nor silently dropped winning/losing episodes. Data policy decides invalid coverage before performance comparisons.

For strategy S and comparator B on identical funded capital, calendar and accounting conventions, let r(S,t) and r(B,t) be simple USD portfolio returns excluding external flows. Define d(t)=r(S,t)-r(B,t), and μ=E[d(t)] over the admitted evaluation distribution. The primary economic null is μ ≤ δ, with **δ ≥ 0** a preregistered minimum incremental effect per reporting interval. δ accounts for the required economic materiality *after* modeled recurring costs; do not charge the same cost twice. Research/acquisition/development costs are reported separately as project payback and sensitivity, not retrospectively hidden in or excluded from the rule to make it pass. No artificial upper bound on profit is imposed.

C1 compares with a frozen passive exposure-matched reference; C3-I compares a joint additive information procedure with the frozen best individual-block procedure; C3-X compares explicit interactions with a nonlinear additive procedure using the same inputs. Each primary claim also requires absolute net performance above accessible USD cash by δ_cash ≥ 0. This prevents declaring the less negative strategy economically successful. C2 is a secondary risk/utility comparison, not another independent alpha claim. Full comparator and selection rules are in the candidate annex.

Cash yield, access/custody, exposure matching, risk constraints and costs must be feasible, not risk-free assumptions about stablecoins. USD 1,000/5,000/10,000 are sensitivity scenarios, not three selectable confirmatory tests or personal limits. Bind one reference scenario before selection; report all prespecified sensitivities without choosing the most favorable capital. A new product/universe/horizon or primary risk metric creates a new comparison and needs budget/admission, not an unrecorded retry.

## 3. Chronological design and availability

Use three chronological layers with disjoint evaluation periods:

1. **Development:** nested past-only walk-forward. Inner folds choose preprocessing, regularization and recipe within the finite envelope. Outer development folds evaluate the entire selection procedure, not the best global fitted model. Outer results cannot tune inner choices within that same claimed OOS track; revising the procedure creates an explicitly exploratory version and consumes the search budget.
2. **Validation:** one later untouched block for admission of the frozen candidate procedures. Freeze all configurations, comparator choices, metric/thresholds and refit schedules before revealing it. A prespecified causal refit may use earlier observations only once labels are available; its algorithm may not change. A failure triggers reject/inconclusive/defer, not retuning against this validation block. If reused for development, record that contamination and require new independent validation.
3. **Final holdout:** one later reserved period, opened once in a single authorized batch for the surviving frozen claims and their references. Validation is not the final test. Final failure never sends the same data back through selection as an independent test.

Do not impose 70/15/15 percentages, universal years, minimum trade counts or fixed bar intervals. The binding partition manifest must contain UTC start/end, reporting interval, warm-up, training origin/length, inner and outer fold boundaries, refit times and label-availability constraints. Choose dates using source coverage, mechanism and precision/power requirements without viewing final returns. If suitable independent coverage is unavailable, wait for separately authorized future data or defer; more overlapping bars do not replace independent regimes.

Sample adequacy is bound **before validation**: specify desired confidence half-width around δ and desired power against a stated economically relevant alternative, using development-only dependence/variance estimates and sensitivity to their uncertainty. Record the required and available number of independent time blocks and held-out regimes; if inadequate, the conclusion is insufficient information. Do not keep extending evaluation until significance. Any preplanned calendar extension must be bounded and approved before observing its outcomes; no sequential peeking is authorized by this protocol.

For training observation i, store feature/event time, latest input availability, decision time, inclusive label endpoint and label-available time. At training cutoff s require every input and label **available strictly before s**. At prediction time only input availability ≤ decision time is allowed; execution occurs after decision plus the documented latency. Never fill at a close that generated the signal before that close could be received.

All assets share fold cutoffs: future observations of another asset cannot enter training. Every fitted operation—normalization, imputation, winsorization, feature screening, knots, factors, graphs, regimes and instrument eligibility learned from values—belongs inside the appropriate training fold. Warm-up uses only past accessible values; insufficient warm-up means no eligible prediction, not backward filling.

### Purge and embargo

Purge training labels/information intervals intersecting the evaluation window; a label ending exactly at evaluation start is excluded. Pre-boundary buffers, if needed, derive from label horizon, feature availability delays, overlap and known dependence, not an arbitrary percentage. Document interval endpoint conventions and demonstrate boundary cases with synthetic checks before financial use.

An embargo after an evaluation block matters only if a design later trains on observations after that block. Strictly past-only training has no such rows: an embargo cannot add protection by name alone. When expanding folds later incorporate previously evaluated observations, require labels available before the new fit; prevent information overlap with the next evaluation. Event/duplicate groups are kept together or purged when they straddle boundaries. Purge/embargo cannot repair revised news, stale quotes, final universes or contaminated pretrained models.

The existing `lab/temporal.py` prepares bounded synthetic half-open partitions and expanding folds. It does not implement nested financial selection, event grouping, a custodian or final unsealing. Its technical tests are historical software evidence, not execution of this protocol.

## 4. Finite search and selection accounting

The candidate annex enumerates **26 base specification slots** for one admitted horizon/universe/execution scenario: eight rule/reference slots and eighteen C3 estimator/ablation slots. The number follows the planned comparisons, not a probability of success. It is a ceiling, not an instruction to run unnecessary models. C4/C5/news/DL do not consume a hidden reserve and have no current trial allocation.

A base slot is not a fit. Before launch enumerate every tuple of recipe, parameters, fold, seed, refit, ablation and stress scenario. The freeze template requires explicit total fit, sensitivity-evaluation, CPU-hour and storage ceilings, plus stop-on-exhaustion. The product cannot be evaluated while these fields are null. Use one deterministic seed schedule per applicable stochastic method initially; extra seeds are prespecified robustness runs and all reported, not seed selection. Identical reproducibility reruns are linked to the original and do not create independent evidence; a repaired implementation is a new version with the failed run retained.

Inner selection optimizes the registered net-return differential subject to common prospective feasibility/risk constraints. Equal scores use lower complexity then stable recipe ID; the numerical tie tolerance must be frozen. Validation/final results never break ties. Report raw counts of every attempt and discretionary choice; no optimistic effective-test count erases manual search. Exhaustion, data unsuitability, absence of a deployable comparator or infeasible precision stops the wave. A material new hypothesis may justify a later prospectively budgeted wave, never reopening the same final data to rescue an old claim.

## 5. Inference and multiplicity

For each of the three possible primary claims (C1, C3-I, C3-X), test both incremental and absolute-cash inequalities. For valid one-sided p-values p_gain and p_cash, use **p_claim=max(p_gain,p_cash)**: both conditions must hold. Across the three planned claims use Holm step-down family-wise control at **α=0.05**, retaining unrun/ineligible claims as p=1 rather than shrinking the family opportunistically. Order p_(1)≤p_(2)≤p_(3); reject successively only while p_(j)≤0.05/(4-j), stopping at first failure. This conventional evidentiary error budget is a methodological proposal for approval, not the probability a strategy is profitable. No alpha is recycled from C2, failed claims or future waves.

Define the same family for the one-time validation screen and the one-time final batch; **only final rejections** support the confirmatory claim. Validation is a gate, not another chance to report success if final fails. Conditional validity still requires prespecified procedures and adequately independent held-out blocks; chronological order alone does not make them independent. A material dependence bridge or structural shift that invalidates inference blocks a positive statistical claim rather than being cured by Holm.

Proposed uncertainty engine: paired stationary time-block bootstrap of the **whole synchronized vector** of candidate/reference net returns and fixed regime labels, with common time indices across assets and strategies. Derive paired differentials after aligned resampling. Retain dependence/overlap via blocks; never resample individual trades or assets independently. Inferential draws do not reconstruct realistic trading paths, order-book fills or drawdown limits. Do not concatenate disjoint folds or omitted gaps into adjacent observations; bind contiguous-segment handling and aggregation weights. Before financial inference, require independent synthetic boundary checks and dependent-null calibration of the implementation; none has been executed for this new method in IV.

For mean-gain testing at the boundary μ=δ, center x_t=d_t−mean(d), generate stationary-bootstrap samples x*, and compare mean(x*) with mean(d)−δ. Use p=(1+# exceedances)/(B+1), with **B=9,999** and seed recorded before outcomes. This is an approximate bootstrap test under its dependence/stationarity conditions, not a distribution-free guarantee. The number gives Monte Carlo granularity 0.0001; it does not create market information. Report simulation uncertainty for p near the decision boundary; do not rerun seeds until passing. Freeze the mean-block-length selector, its training-only calibration window and a finite short/base/long block sensitivity before validation. Evaluate all; do not choose the one yielding significance. If results depend materially on the block choice, classify fragility/inconclusive under the preregistered rule.

Report paired confidence intervals for effect sizes with clearly stated coverage. Marginal 95% intervals are **descriptive**, not simultaneous evidence for three claims; decisions use the intersection/Holm tests. Bootstrap small-sample, heavy-tail and nonstationarity failures must be examined; no admissible uncertainty estimate means no positive statistical conclusion. The [method evidence](method-evidence.md) documents the verified software-method scope and limits, not a financial replication.

DSR is a secondary diagnostic only when moments, serial dependence treatment, frequency, full trial history and effective-trial assumptions can be justified. Report total trials and sensitivity of effective counts, never label DSR a future-profit probability. PBO/CSCV is deferred in the first wave: it needs a complete aligned alternative-return matrix and does not reproduce past-only training. White/SPA and Lo original texts remain partially accessed in the scientific record; no unverified formula is attributed to them or used as a fallback to obtain significance. No optional method is tried merely because the primary result fails.

## 6. Metrics, regime analysis and stress

| Metric | Prospective convention / invalid cases |
|---|---|
| Primary effect | Mean net USD simple-return differential per common calendar interval; cash gate separately; δ and δ_cash bound before evaluation |
| CAGR | `(E_end/E_start)^(365.25/calendar_days)-1` for positive equity, with external flows removed by time-weighted subperiod returns; undefined for nonpositive equity, which triggers failure analysis |
| Sharpe | Mean excess return / standard deviation at the reporting interval; report serial dependence and uncertainty. Naive square-root annualization is not a significance test; any adjusted annualization must be specified and justified |
| Sortino | Excess over a preregistered minimum acceptable return, divided by root mean squared downside over **all** intervals; zero downside ⇒ undefined, not infinite success |
| Drawdown/Calmar | Peak-to-trough marked USD equity loss on the full calendar; Calmar=CAGR/absolute maximum drawdown when defined. Simulated percentiles are not loss guarantees |
| Profit factor/expectancy | Net matched closed-trade P&L using preregistered attribution/lot matching; profit factor=sum gains/absolute sum losses; expectancy=net closed P&L/trade count. No losses/no trades ⇒ undefined respective metrics; open P&L reported separately |
| Turnover/exposure | Turnover=Σ absolute traded USD notional / prior equity (one-way convention, buys and sells counted); gross/net exposure and time invested, idle cash, fees/funding and fixed costs separately |
| Distribution/tails | Quantiles, skew/kurtosis only if estimable, loss concentration and expected shortfall at a prespecified level with effective tail count; do not report precise tail inference from a few observations |

No metric gets silently replaced when undefined. C2's primary risk objective and permitted return sacrifice must be bound separately before evaluation; reduced exposure alone is not alpha. Cash and buy-and-hold references share timing, costs and funding conventions where applicable, with native and training-calibrated risk/exposure-matched results distinguished.

Regimes are **causal contextual labels**, not optimized subperiods: lagged volatility/liquidity and past trend thresholds estimated only on training; freeze the small set and cutoffs before evaluation. Ex post crisis narratives may be descriptive but cannot become predictive regime inputs. Report each prespecified regime, support size and uncertainty, including empty/weak regimes and adverse periods. Regime-level p-values are exploratory unless separately budgeted in advance; do not manufacture independent replications from correlated assets/folds.

Stress axes are declared in the data annex: fees, spread/impact, decision/fill delay, missing/stale data, liquidity/capacity, conversion/depeg and relevant financing/margin. Specify base/adverse values and source/rationale before selection; use finite one-factor changes plus one justified joint adverse scenario, not a Cartesian optimization. Report break-even cost/capacity and all planned stress failures. Operational feasibility gates, maximum acceptable research drawdown/scenario loss and capital feasibility are unresolved bindings, not the user's accepted live limits.

## 7. Holdout custody and amendment protocol

Holdout metadata must include source/version/license, admitted universe rules, event/availability clocks, UTC interval, row coverage policy, content hashes, code/lock hashes, all frozen recipes/comparators, inference/budget, custodian, access log and approval reference. Hashes assert content identity, not an unseen or truthful source. Historical periods whose performance was inspected through publications/manual charts are declared prior knowledge; if materially used to design the procedure, they are not pristine confirmatory evidence. Prefer an otherwise unexamined later period; prospective collection remains separately authorized.

Future opening requires all readiness fields bound, user's V authorization, verification of permissions/custody, approved frozen bundle digest and one opening request with batch IDs. A separate reviewer/custodian should release only that batch; for a personal single-host deployment this is a procedural safeguard, **not** isolation from the OS owner. The current app denies final payload to every role and has no financial unseal command. Do not bypass it via direct SQLite/files to perform an evaluation. A future admitted adapter must implement and test one-time audited access before use.

Use phases of the opening record: requested → authorized → started → completed/failed, plus timestamp, actor and scope. Any financial preview, partial report, debug summary or AI prompt containing reserved values consumes the exposure and is logged; a failed run does not automatically restore unseen status. A deterministic infrastructure retry is allowed only if no new scientific choice is made, evidence/access reviewed and a new attempt linked; if results informed repair/selection, retire confirmatory status and require new independent data. A hash alone cannot erase knowledge.

Changes before outcomes: append rationale, changed fields, superseded version, expected multiplicity/resource impact and approval responsibility. After inspecting development outcomes: exploratory amendment with its consumed budget. After validation/final exposure: preserve the result and require a new independent evaluation for revised claims. Never modify historical configuration, remove negative runs or relabel an old test independent.

## 8. Records, outcomes and next approval

Each planned experiment record contains hypothesis/version/null; recipe/comparator; intended role (exploratory/validation/confirmatory/sensitivity/technical); data/clock/universe and split digest; code/environment; capital/risk/costs; exact budget and consumed attempts; seeds/refits; metrics/inference; predeclared falsifiers; permissions; timestamps; artifacts; result including error/negative/inconclusive; and review/next-action reasons. Link proposals/inferences separately from observed evidence. The existing technical record is useful infrastructure but not proof this financial schema or engine already exists.

Outcomes are: **technically invalid** (retain and repair only with contamination review), **not evaluable/deferred** (data/resources/access), **inconclusive** (precision/assumptions/fragility), **evidence against the specified economically material claim** (valid upper bounds below δ or clear cost infeasibility), or **supports the scoped claim** (all prospective economic, inference, stress, risk and integrity conditions pass). Failure to reject is not proof no advantage exists. Supporting a claim authorizes neither capital nor prospective/live operation. Stop without a winner when the registered candidates lack sufficient evidence.

The remaining decisions and acceptance mapping are explicit in [acceptance](acceptance.md). No further general literature review, UX implementation or compatibility project is a dependency of IV. A request for V approval must specify its data/engine admission work, budget and exact executable frozen protocol; approval of this methodological document alone must not silently fill its missing parameters or run experiments.
